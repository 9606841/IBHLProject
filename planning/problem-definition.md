## User or client: Who might use this solution?
* high school students
* college students
* working adults
## Problem: What difficulty, inefficiency, or unmet need exists?
There's a lack of free, accessible, a flexible planning applications for students and working adults. Furthermore, health tracking is something that isn't intuitive or included in many planning applications, causing an unmet need for those who work to be efficient but take care of themselves simultaneously.
## Proposed solution: What could the program allow the user to do?
It would allow the user to use a planner that includes a calendar, journal, task manager, and more with customizable color themes and formats.
## Success criteria: Write four to six specific, testable statements.
* Must have an intuitive UIX
* Must have some level of customizability
* Must have effective health tracking method
* Must be accessible for those with disabilities to whatever extent is feasible
## Investigation: Identify one question you must research or ask the intended user.
What's something you'd like to see in planning applications that isn't prolific in most apps?
